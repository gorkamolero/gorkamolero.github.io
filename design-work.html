openroadie

Roadie · 2026 · Open source

openroadie is the open-source context layer that came out of Roadie, and I designed and built its interfaces. It maps how a company's services, repos and on-call connect, so agents can answer questions about them. The app runs on @roadiehq/ui, a design system I co-led, and these screens come from a local install with made-up demo data.
