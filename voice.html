Voice, sound and agents

I've produced music since I was a teenager, studied sound engineering and mixing, and have built software for fourteen years. Most of what I make now has a voice in it: an AI narrator people play a story with, fifty-minute episodes narrated for my YouTube channel, and a carving in the woods that talks back.

Voices you can talk to

The Pulse

Live · 2025–2026 · Interactive fiction

A reimagining of narrative. A novel has a timeline of events and a whole world around it. The Pulse keeps the world, the characters and the places, and lets go of the timeline: you can do whatever you want, in any order, and the story follows you. It's very immersive. The narrator for Shadow Over Innsmouth is a clone of Orson Welles, running as an ElevenLabs agent.

Talking Statues

Prototype · 2026 · Voice agents

Unconditional Carving, a studio in Missouri, has started an experimental project: carvings you come across in the forest and can actually talk to. The idea is that you wander the woods, find these spirits one by one, and have a conversation with each. I built the first one, a jackalope with a Raspberry Pi, a microphone and speakers inside, connected to an ElevenLabs agent. It's location-aware, and it sleeps until someone wakes it up.

Cybertantra

YouTube · 2026 · Long-form and Shorts

The latest iteration of Saga, my long-running experiment with gen-AI pipelines, and my first attempt at long-form. The art comes from OpenAI image generation, and a virtual camera moves across each image, following whatever the current sentence is about. The narration is Eleven v4, generated in chunks with a warm-up line before each one and stitched back together, so the voice holds for a fifty-minute episode. The score is Eleven Music, and everything is cut in Remotion.

Agents

I also build agent systems, for voice and for code. On my creative portfolio, an ElevenLabs agent answers questions about my work out loud, and The Pulse's narrator is an agent you can talk to directly. At Roadie I built the AI infrastructure, the MCP server and much of the context layer for agents.

Koda

2026 · Tauri, React, Node, Liveblocks, Codex, Claude

A desktop app for working collaboratively with coding agents. A Codex or Claude session runs on your machine, and a teammate opens it from their own Koda with view or control access. They see the same transcript and tool calls live, and with control they can message the agent too. Any action that needs approval stops for Approve once or Deny right in the chat. Every flow is proven against the real services with Playwright, from a clean clone.

Claude-on-Discord

2026 · Claude Code, Discord, Bun, SQLite

Claude Code run from Discord. Each channel is a working lane with its own repo, model, session and system prompt, and each thread can branch into its own git worktree. The agent's replies, thinking, tool calls and cost stream live into the conversation, with buttons to interrupt or stop it, and a supervisor keeps the bridge alive and recovers it after a crash.
