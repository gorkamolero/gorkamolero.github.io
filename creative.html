Creative work

My name is Gorka Molero. I love writing, music, art, technology, media and spirituality, and I spend my days trying to put all of this together. I like to push AI into places where it looks like it shouldn't work yet, such as long-form video, spirituality, music production, unscripted video games and statues you can talk to. Here's a selection of personal projects.

Cybertantra

YouTube · 2026Long-form and Shorts

The latest iteration of Saga, my long-running experiment with gen-AI pipelines, and my first attempt at long-form. The art comes from OpenAI image generation, and a virtual camera moves across each image, following whatever the current sentence is about. The narration is Eleven v4, generated in chunks with a warm-up line before each one and stitched back together, so the voice holds for a fifty-minute episode. The score is Eleven Music, and everything is cut in Remotion.

Here's a short version. You'll find the full episode and the Shorts below.

Talking carvings

Prototype · 2026Voice agents

Unconditional Carving, a studio in Missouri, has started an experimental project: carvings you come across in the forest and can actually talk to. The idea is that you wander the woods, find these spirits one by one, and have a conversation with each. I built the first one, a jackalope with a Raspberry Pi, a microphone and speakers inside, connected to an ElevenLabs agent. It's location-aware, and it sleeps until someone wakes it up.

A visitor asks for a story about stars. The jackalope invents one on the spot.

Carvingen

Tool · 2026AI image to 3D

For the same studio, a canvas for designing carvings with AI. A carver drops in references, generates image variations, branches from any result, and turns the chosen image into a 3D model to inspect from every side. The images come from fal's models and the 3D from Meshy.

The Pulse

Live · 2025–2026Interactive fiction

A reimagining of narrative. A novel has a timeline of events and a whole world around it. The Pulse keeps the world, the characters and the places, and lets go of the timeline: you can do whatever you want, in any order, and the story follows you. It's very immersive. The narrator for Shadow Over Innsmouth is a clone of Orson Welles, running as an ElevenLabs agent.

In Shadow Over Innsmouth, a player shows the stationmaster a wax seal, and the narrator runs with it.

Point-and-click, unscripted

Game · 2025Open dialogue

A LucasArts-style adventure with the dialogue tree left open. Type anything to anyone, and they answer in character, in their own voice. Next I want to leave the decisions open too, like in The Pulse, so you make your own way as you go: say you want to go to the bus station, and one is created in the style of the story. I've started on that, and it's on hold for now.

In the bar, a player ignores the dialogue menu and types their own lines.

Solderpunk

Web app · 2026Music production

AI-assisted synth explorers. Hover any control on a synth's panel to learn what it does, follow guided tutorials, drive the real hardware over MIDI, and ask questions of a cited knowledge base for each instrument. It covers seven instruments, from the Moog Minitaur and Grandmother to the DSI Tempest and the Empress ZOIA.

Podcast studio

System · 2026Remote video podcast

A studio for a video podcast I co-host remotely. I wanted to build my own software, at feature parity with Riverside and others. One command starts everything: the call, a 4K program feed, every track, and the slides on my co-host's screen. If his connection drops, the studio brings him back and fills the gap from a backup of his own mic. An automated test runs the whole studio and checks the recordings: every voice, frozen frames and missing tracks.

The Last Alchemist

YouTube Shorts · 2023–2024Automated pipeline

The first iteration of Saga, the project that ended up being Cybertantra. I set out to build a completely automated channel where all I'd give it was the editorial line, and I wanted to prove it could grow exponentially. In 34 days it made and uploaded 205 Shorts, gained 1,000 subscribers and drew 135K views, with no paid promotion, at under $1 a Short. It's an old project on old models. Once it had proved the point, I stopped and waited for the models to be ready. The second iteration, in 2024, was a writers' room of agents for long-form scripts: brief, research, outline, draft, critique and final script.
